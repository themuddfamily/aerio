# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Automatic archiving into Local Folders: an opt-in policy per account (Account settings › Automatic archiving) moves mail received more than N days or months ago from the whole account or selected folders into a Local Folders folder every day, week, or month, keeping starred/flagged, unread, pinned, draft, or sent messages and chosen folders on the server if asked, with optional per-account, per-year, and source-path subfolders. **Preview** shows the count, size, exclusions, and the oldest messages with their destinations; **Run now** starts a run. Each run is a durable job: every message is copied byte for byte, verified by re-reading its size and SHA-256 (and attachment count), and only then removed from the server with a message-level operation (Gmail `messages.trash`, Graph move to Deleted Items or `permanentDelete`, IMAP UID move to Trash or delete), so newer replies in the same conversation stay. Moving to Trash is the default; Gmail cannot delete permanently with Aerio's grant. A full disk pauses the run without removing server copies, failures keep the server copy with a reason, and interrupted runs resume after a restart without duplicate copies. Runs happen while Aerio or its tray process runs and catch up on the next launch; policies and history are in profile backups ([automatic archiving](docs/automatic-archiving.md)).
- Pinned mail conversations: **Pin to top** / **Unpin** in the conversation context menu, the selection and reader toolbars, the command palette, and Quick Action steps keeps conversations above the date-ordered list in every folder and the unified view, most recently pinned first, so new mail never reshuffles them. Pins are local-only `pin`/`unpin` actions that are never sent to a provider, are keyed by account and thread id, survive restarts and resyncs, appear in profile backups, and undo exactly through the usual Undo toast; list cursors carry the pin partition so pagination stays stable. Stars and importance are unaffected. See [Pinned mail conversations](docs/mail-pinning.md).
- Rich Notes: notes are edited with headings, lists, links, tables, quotes, and the mail composer's formatting toolbar, plus inline pictures (insert, paste, or drop PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP/BMP up to 5 MB). Pictures are stored as managed inline note attachments and referenced by a stable id, never as data URLs in the note. Pasted HTML is sanitized with the composer allowlist (no scripts, handlers, or remote images), and the main process sanitizes every saved body again. Plain-text notes open unchanged and gain a rich body only when edited; `content` remains the plain-text projection that search, previews, and older versions use. Portable local-data backups carry inline pictures, and older plain-text backups still restore. See [rich notes](docs/rich-notes.md).
- S/MIME mail (RFC 8551) using node-forge 1.4.0: Sign and Encrypt with S/MIME in the composer (detached `multipart/signed` with SHA-256, and `application/pkcs7-mime` enveloped data with AES-256-CBC), with a recipient-certificate check, a choice of certificate per address, and a per-sender default. A message uses S/MIME or OpenPGP, never both. The conversation view verifies signed and opaque-signed mail with Aerio's own CMS verifier and decrypts on request, showing the signer's issuer chain, validity, key usage, email match, and that revocation was not checked. Certificates from valid signed mail are saved for replies. Settings › S/MIME certificates imports PKCS#12 (.p12/.pfx) and .cer/.crt/.pem/.der files, marks roots or certificates trusted, and exports certificates or a password-protected PKCS#12. Private keys are stored as passphrase-encrypted PKCS#8 inside a store wrapped with OS secure storage, left out of profile backups, and forgotten after 10 minutes or when Aerio locks. Missing, expired, untrusted, or wrong-usage certificates, a locked signing certificate, or a failed encryption stop the send and keep the draft. See `docs/smime-mail.md`.
- Matrix chat connector (behind a closed gate): an isolated `matrix-js-sdk` 43 service with Rust crypto per account, an OS-encrypted vault, an encrypted local cache, a durable outbox that never falls back to plaintext, and a Chat view with rooms and unread counts, encrypted and unencrypted indicators, the timeline (undecryptable messages stay visible with a reason), replies, edits, reactions, deletion, encrypted attachments up to 25 MiB, local-history search, invitations, mute and notification-preview controls, emoji device verification, recovery-key setup and restore, device trust, and per-account retention, clear history and removal. Chat IPC refuses calls from the service and every command while Aerio is locked; notifications are generic by default and open the room after unlocking. Chat appears in navigation only when secure storage, the packaged service, a recorded crypto-store self-test and hosted two-device interoperability evidence all pass; only the last can be waived, by the developer-only `AERIO_CHAT_PREVIEW=1`. Hosted interoperability is not yet recorded, so Chat stays hidden. `npm run test:chat` checks the gate in real Electron. See `docs/chat-matrix-connector.md`.

- Meeting invitations: messages with iTIP `text/calendar` parts show the meeting, its organizer, guests, rooms, and responses, with Accept, Tentative, and Decline, an optional comment, and a proposed new time where the provider supports one. Meetings in a synced Google calendar are answered with `events.patch` and `sendUpdates=all`, Outlook meetings with Graph accept/tentativelyAccept/decline (including `proposedNewTime`), and everything else with an iTIP REPLY or COUNTER mailed to the organizer, with accepted meetings copied into a local Invitations calendar. Updates are tracked by UID, SEQUENCE, and DTSTAMP, so duplicate invitations and out-of-date updates or requests after a cancellation are explained instead of acted on, and cancellations can remove the local copy. Events now keep their organizer, attendee roles (chair, required, optional, non-participant), rooms and resources, response status, and SEQUENCE across Google, Microsoft, and ICS import/export; the event editor has optional-attendee and rooms-and-resources fields, Google meetings with guests notify them of changes, and synced meetings organized by others can be answered from Calendar.
- Online meetings: add Google Meet (conferenceData createRequest with conferenceDataVersion=1) or Microsoft Teams (isOnlineMeeting/teamsForBusiness) to Google and Microsoft calendar events from the event editor, and join them with a Join button that opens https links on meet.google.com, teams.microsoft.com, and teams.live.com directly and asks before opening any other host. Calendars report which meetings they allow (Google allowedConferenceSolutionTypes, Graph allowedOnlineMeetingProviders), so personal Microsoft accounts and Workspace domains without Meet explain why no meeting can be added. A stable request id (also the Google event id and the Graph transactionId) and a check for existing meetings before creating keep retries from creating duplicates. Unrelated edits never send conference fields, and Microsoft edits no longer rewrite the event body unless the notes changed. Zoom and Webex have a connector interface and are reported as not connected. See `docs/online-meetings.md`.
- Contact duplicates and communication history: Contacts › Find duplicates suggests likely duplicates scored by exact email (dots ignored for Gmail only), normalized phone, and name similarity, with a confidence and the reasons, and never merges on its own. Contacts in the same address book or provider account can be merged after a field-by-field preview that keeps every email, phone, address, website, and group; provider merges update the kept contact through the usual revision-checked write and delete the duplicate. Every merge is journaled locally and can be undone (a deleted provider duplicate is recreated as a new contact). Contacts from different owners can be linked on this PC instead, and pairs can be marked as not duplicates. The contact pane now shows where the record is stored and its recent messages and attachments with any of its (and its linked contacts') addresses across the selected mail accounts, with paging, archived-account labels, and click-through to the message.
- OpenPGP mail (PGP/MIME, RFC 3156) using OpenPGP.js 6: Sign and Encrypt toggles in the composer, with a recipient-key check and a choice of key when an address has several; automatic signature verification and on-request decryption in the conversation view, including decrypted attachments and read-only inline PGP; and an OpenPGP key manager in Settings for generating, importing (armored or binary), exporting, deleting, and manually verifying keys. Private keys are kept passphrase-encrypted inside a keyring wrapped with OS secure storage, are left out of profile backups, and unlocked keys are forgotten after 10 minutes or when Aerio locks. Missing, expired, or revoked keys, a locked signing key, or a failed encryption stop the send and keep the draft with a clear error. Encrypted drafts are not uploaded to the provider.
- CalDAV and CardDAV calendar and contact accounts for iCloud, Fastmail, Nextcloud, and other servers, added in Settings › Calendar and contacts accounts with an app-specific password kept in OS secure storage. Discovery uses `.well-known` and `current-user-principal`. Read-only, shared, and tasks-only collections are recognized from privileges and component sets. Sync is incremental with `sync-collection`, falling back to ctag and ETag comparison, and fetches with multiget. Edits are queued durably offline and sent with `If-Match`/`If-None-Match`, keeping server properties Aerio does not edit. A `412` or deleted-on-server conflict goes to review and is never overwritten silently. HTTPS is required except on loopback. Profile backups include the cache and queue but never the passwords, and restored writes are held for review ([CalDAV and CardDAV](docs/caldav-carddav.md)).
- Scheduling assistant in the event editor: a free/busy timeline for the organizer, required and optional attendees, and resources, using Google Calendar `freeBusy` or Microsoft Graph `getSchedule` depending on the organizer's calendar. Unavailable data (external domains, unshared calendars, permission gaps, provider errors) is hatched as unknown and never counted as free; Outlook working hours and your own working hours shape the next suggested slots; times show in your zone with an optional second zone. New Google connections request `calendar.freebusy`; older Google accounts are offered a reconnect from the assistant. See `docs/scheduling-assistant.md`.
- Calendar recurrence and time zones: every occurrence of a repeating local or imported event appears in month, week, day, and agenda views; a repeat editor covers intervals, weekdays, nth/last weekdays, yearly rules, and end dates or counts; rules Aerio cannot edit are kept read-only rather than dropped. Saving or deleting a repeating event asks for this event, this and following events, or all events, on local calendars, Google (recurringEventId/originalStartTime), and Microsoft (seriesMasterId/patternedRecurrence). Events keep named time zones and all-day dates, and week and day views can show a secondary time zone.
- Richer message composition: font, size, text and highlight color, strikethrough, alignment, numbered lists, tables, and inline images (insert, paste, or drop) in the composer. Pasted HTML is sanitized with a strict tag/attribute/CSS allowlist that strips Word and Office markup, remote images, scripts, and handlers; Ctrl+Shift+V pastes plain text. Inline images are embedded in the draft, survive autosave and reopening, and are sent as multipart/related Content-ID parts next to the plain-text alternative for Gmail, Microsoft, and SMTP. Rich signatures and templates now keep tables, fonts, colors, and safe inline styles when sent.
- Optional AI assistance, off by default: summarize an email or get suggested replies from its menu, draft or rephrase from the composer, and ask for a search in plain language. Works with the Anthropic Messages API or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint (including local Ollama). The API key is encrypted with OS secure storage and never reaches the renderer; Settings › AI assistance lists exactly what each feature sends and lets you turn features and sender details on or off. Email content is passed as delimited untrusted data, the model has no tools, output is shown as plain-text previews, and inserting text, opening a reply, or running a proposed search (validated by the search parser) always needs a click. Requests can be cancelled and time out after 60 seconds. Translation and manual meeting/task creation are unchanged.
- QuickText snippets and whole-message mail templates with subject, sanitized HTML, and managed attachments; search, edit, and insert them from the composer without losing the editor selection, and back them up with local data or as a standalone template file.
- Sender identities: Gmail send-as aliases and Microsoft mailbox aliases are discovered automatically, IMAP and shared-mailbox addresses can be added manually, and each identity has a Reply-To, a default flag, and separate HTML signatures for new messages and replies. The composer swaps only the marked signature block when From changes, drafts keep their identity and Reply-To, and provider send-as refusals keep the message as a failed draft with a clear explanation.
- Watch for replies: turn it on in the composer or from a sent message’s menu, with an optional deadline. Aerio matches replies by Message-ID references and the provider thread for the sending account, notifies from the background or tray when a recipient answers or the deadline passes without a reply, and lists active and past watches under Mail › Reply watches, where they can be cancelled.
- Full-profile backup and restore in Settings: daily, 3-day, or weekly snapshots (or on demand) of the mail cache, drafts, queued changes, rules, templates, local Tasks/Notes/Contacts, connected-task cache, and preferences, using SQLite's online backup API with per-file SHA-256 checksums and keep-N retention. Restore verifies and previews the backup, migrates and stages it, then swaps it in on restart with automatic rollback if the swap fails or is interrupted. Credentials are never included, restored outgoing and scheduled sends are held in Drafts for review, connected task writes wait for review, and accounts reauthorize.
- User-defined mail Quick Actions: save ordered sequences of mail actions (read, star, label, move, archive, Trash, and more) and run them on the selection from the toolbars, the conversation context menu, the command palette, or Ctrl+Shift+1–9. Steps use the queued operation pipeline with Undo; account-specific destinations are checked at run time and skipped or partly updated accounts are reported. Undo of several queued changes now reverts them newest first, and queued operations created in the same millisecond reach the provider in insertion order.
- Local Folders and mail import: a provider-independent, never-synchronized Local Folders account with writable, nestable folders (create, rename, move, delete to Trash, read/star/move/Trash with Undo). Import MBOX (mboxrd or mboxo, streamed in bounded memory) and EML files or whole directory trees, including Thunderbird `.sbd` and Apple Mail `.mbox` exports, keeping the folder hierarchy, dates, Status/X-Mozilla-Status flags, original MIME, and attachments. Imports run in the mail worker with progress, pause, cancel, a 64 MB per-message limit, a disk-space pause, and resumable cursors that survive restarts without duplicating messages. Duplicates (identical content hash or same Message-ID) are held for review and can be imported or discarded. Conversations can be copied or moved into Local Folders from any account, including offline archives (copy only). Outlook PST and OST files (ANSI and Unicode, including compressible encryption) are imported through pst-extractor in the mail worker: their folder tree, sender and recipients, dates, read and flag state, plain/HTML bodies (HTML de-encapsulated from compressed RTF when that is all there is), attachments, inline images and attached Outlook items are rebuilt as deterministic MIME and go through the same progress, pause/resume, limits and duplicate review, resuming at the PST folder and item. Calendar, contact and task folders are skipped, and high-encryption PSTs and .msg files are rejected with an explanation.
- Attachment-content search: text from PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and plain-text or HTML attachments is extracted on the device in an isolated worker with memory, time, and size limits and indexed with the message, so `attachment:` and ordinary searches find words inside attachments as well as their filenames. The reader notes attachments that can't be searched because they are password-protected, too large, or unreadable; indexing resumes incrementally and an account's extracted text is removed when the account is deleted.
- Cloud attachments (opt-in): share a file from the composer as a Google Drive (`drive.file`) or OneDrive (`Files.ReadWrite`) link instead of attaching it. The sharing scope (anyone with the link, people in the organization, or only the recipients; view or edit) is shown before upload, attachments over the provider limit get a "share as cloud link" offer, and ordinary attachments are unchanged. Uploads run in the main process in resumable chunks with progress and cancel, resume after a restart from an encrypted session record or are cleared when they can't, and links Aerio created are re-checked before sending so deleted files, removed permissions, and expired links are reported. The storage permission is added by an explicit reconnect that keeps existing grants.
- Advanced mail rules: Cc, header, attachment, size, age, importance, and folder/label conditions; “except if” exceptions; and outgoing rules that run on new Sent mail. A per-message ledger keyed by Message-ID makes sure a message is never processed twice, including after resyncs and IMAP folder moves. Existing rules keep their stored format and behavior.
- Server rules: upload a rule as a Gmail filter (`users.settings.filters`) or an Outlook inbox rule (Graph `messageRules`), list, update, turn off (Outlook), or remove provider rules, and see which conditions or actions can't map to the server. An uploaded rule runs only on the server. Accounts without the new `gmail.settings.basic` or `MailboxSettings.ReadWrite` permission are asked to reconnect.
- Mail merge: from the composer, expand selected contacts or a pasted address list/CSV into separate single-recipient messages with `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{company}}`, custom-column variables, and `{{name|fallback}}` defaults. Step through a per-recipient preview, confirm the count, privacy, and conservative per-provider per-minute and daily limits, then follow per-recipient progress with pause, cancel, and skip. Values are HTML-escaped in the body and cannot add subject headers. Progress is stored in SQLite and resumes after a restart; a send interrupted mid-submission (or that times out) is marked for review and never resent automatically, and the merge waits until you choose Resend or Skip. Rate-limit replies back off automatically.

### Changed

- Incoming rules no longer run on new drafts or Sent-only copies; use an outgoing rule for sent mail. “Equals” on To or Cc now also matches a single recipient’s address or name.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-08-08

### Added

- Unread message counts for every mail folder and connected account.
- Animated expansion and collapse for individual messages in a conversation.
- Recurring local tasks that schedule their next occurrence, plus user-created task lists.
- Configurable Calendar reminders and Microsoft Calendar event creation, editing, and deletion.
- Automatic Calendar and Contacts refresh every 15 minutes.
- Editable local Contacts alongside cached provider contacts.
- Create, edit, and delete Google and Microsoft provider contacts after reconnecting for write access.
- Incremental Google Calendar/People sync tokens and Microsoft Calendar/Contacts delta links with expired-cursor recovery.
- Validated export and restore for local Contacts, Tasks, and Notes.
- Managed Notes attachments with safe local copies, open/remove controls, search, cleanup, and portable backup/restore.
- An optional local passphrase app lock at launch, on tray close, or on demand with Ctrl-L.
- Real Help and What’s New panels, and Ctrl-K searches that reach the Mail view.

### Changed

- Chat is no longer shown in the v1 module rail while its transport and security model remain undecided.
- Tray settings now explain their relationship to scheduled sending, snooze, rules, and background synchronization.
- Draft saves detect stale Aerio editor revisions and Gmail or Microsoft drafts changed by another client, then offer to preserve the stale edit as a separate copy.

### Fixed

- Folder and account unread badges now use account-aware aggregate counts.
- Rapid consecutive draft saves now receive distinct, monotonic revision timestamps.
